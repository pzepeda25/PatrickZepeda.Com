# Design System

## Direction

The site behaves like a director's working studio translated through modern dimensional illustration: cinematic, tactile, precise, and lightly imperfect. Nostalgia comes from familiar creative objects and material details rather than decorative retro UI.

## Color Strategy

Committed palette with true ink and signal red. The supplied warm studio photograph carries the neutral warmth.

- Ink: `#191816`
- Signal Red: `#C83C2F`
- True Off-White: `#F3F2ED`
- Muted Metal: `#9A978E`
- Screen Green: `#71876D`, used only for status details

## Typography

- Display: condensed grotesque with hard editorial confidence
- Body: neutral humanist sans with high readability
- Production labels: restrained monospace, never used as the dominant brand voice

## Layout

- Dimensional object illustrations with softened outlines and separation motion
- Full-bleed photography used as contrast, not the entire visual language
- Asymmetric editorial compositions
- One dominant idea per viewport
- Sharp corners and physical labels
- No repeated card grids, numbered section scaffolds, or decorative interface chrome

## Motion

- One cinematic first-load reveal
- Purposeful image crops and title transitions
- Quiet tactile hover and active states
- Full reduced-motion fallback
